Privacy notice

How this dealership website preview handles information, and what must be completed for a live dealership.

Draft template · Effective date, legal business name, mailing address, privacy email, and operating jurisdictions must be supplied before publication.

Information you provide

Vehicle inquiries and appointment requests may include your name, email, phone number, preferred contact method, vehicle interests, purchase timing, trade-in interest, and message. Do not include Social Security numbers, driver’s license numbers, bank details, or other sensitive financial information in these general forms.

How requests are handled

The preview labels each form’s delivery behavior. An unconnected demo form downloads a request to your device. A configured email option opens a message for you to review and send. A connected dealership endpoint may transmit the form to the dealership and its service provider. The live notice must identify the actual recipient, purposes, service providers, and retention practices.

Local storage and external content

This preview uses browser storage for preferences such as language, saved selections, artwork customization, and acknowledgment of the privacy notice. Uploaded customization images are stored on this device when saved successfully. Clearing site data removes these saved preferences. Some photographs and other assets load from external hosts; those hosts receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address and browser request details. Their practices are governed by their own policies.

No optional analytics or advertising permission is requested by this notice. A live deployment must inventory its cookies, pixels, analytics, CRM tools, and embedded services, disclose the actual practices, and implement any required consent and opt-out controls before enabling those services.

Sharing, security and retention

Before launch, the dealership must describe which information is shared with service providers, lenders, affiliates, or other recipients; why it is shared; how long it is retained; and how it is protected. This draft makes no unsupported promise that information is never shared or sold.

Your requests and choices

Privacy rights depend on your location and the dealership’s practices. The final notice must explain applicable access, correction, deletion, and opt-out rights, verification procedures, and an operational request method. Contact: [Dealership privacy email and mailing address]. This placeholder is not a working request channel.

Financing privacy notices

A general website privacy notice does not replace a financial privacy notice that may be required when arranging or providing financing. The dealership must provide applicable financial privacy disclosures at the appropriate stage of the transaction.

Updates

Publish the effective date and describe material changes when this notice is updated. Revisit this notice whenever data collection, vendors, or dealership practices change.
