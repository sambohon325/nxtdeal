Financing & vehicle disclosures

Important information for independent and Buy Here Pay Here dealership websites.

Draft template · These general disclosures require review against the dealership’s state, licenses, actual fees, advertised offers, lending model, and sales practices.

Payment examples

Review the cash price, down payment, APR, payment amount, payment frequency, term, amount financed, and total of payments for the actual offer. Estimate results can change when taxes, fees, optional products, trade equity, or other inputs change. Weekly and biweekly figures may use approximate conversion methods and are not a substitute for a contract payment schedule.

Approval and in-house financing

Financing is subject to applicable eligibility and verification requirements. Buy Here Pay Here describes a financing arrangement where the dealership may extend or service credit; it does not mean guaranteed approval, no credit review, or a particular rate. The dealership must accurately disclose its actual process and any required licenses.

Fees and optional products

Request an itemized total and review any dealer documentation fee, government fees, taxes, and selected optional products. The live site must identify fees and offer conditions as required for the dealership’s jurisdiction. Do not rely on a general footer to correct incomplete vehicle or financing advertisements.

Used vehicles and warranties

Read the vehicle-specific Buyers Guide and written warranty terms. “As-is” availability and the effect of warranty disclaimers depend on state law and the transaction. This site does not declare all vehicles sold without warranties.

Event offers

Each promotion must state its applicable dates, eligible vehicles, material conditions, and any limits on combining offers. A vehicle’s appearance in an event does not automatically change its advertised price or payment estimate.

Credit applications and agreements

Use an appropriate secure process for sensitive credit application information. Obtain any required credit-report authorization and provide applicable financial privacy and transaction disclosures. Contact forms and website notice acknowledgments do not replace those steps.
